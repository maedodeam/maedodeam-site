Escritório 17h58

It's 5:58 PM. The bus leaves at 6:00. Get from your desk to the exit before a manager hands you one more quick task.

A third-person stealth comedy about the most dangerous two minutes of the workday. Managers patrol, coworkers snitch, the elevator breaks down.

We designed and built it end to end — gameplay, AI, adaptive music, localization and a cross-platform build pipeline — and we're getting it ready for Steam on PC.

P—02 · SaaS · Business platform · MVP

Cotaria

  • SaaS
  • Business systems
  • Backend
  • Frontend
  • Cloud
  • Product development

Hairy financial rules, turned into software that does the math.

Cotaria is a platform for running consórcio groups — Brazil's pooled-credit model, where members pay into a shared fund and receive credit over time.

It brings operations, administration and the member experience into a single product, built to replace spreadsheets and manual work with software where every number can be traced.

03 / AboutThe person behind it

Built by experience. Driven by curiosity.

MAEDODEAM was founded by Roger Maedo, a software engineer with about twenty years of building systems that run in production.

Most of that time went into the hard, invisible parts: backends, architecture, integrations, distributed and enterprise systems — and leading the engineering behind them.

MAEDODEAM is where that experience gets to build things of its own: independent products, games, experiments, and collaborations on problems that are actually interesting.

Small on purpose. Fewer layers, more things that actually ship.
